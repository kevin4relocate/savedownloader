Technical support

If a supported public Instagram, Douyin, or TikTok URL fails, include the platform, the public URL format, the exact error message, and your browser/device type. Do not send passwords, login cookies, payment information, or private URLs.

Email contact

Email contact@savedownloader.com

SaveDownloader no longer uses a public GitHub issue tracker as its primary support channel. Email is preferred so reports that may contain URLs or other contextual information are not published publicly.

What to include in a support request

  • A short description of what you expected to happen.
  • The error text shown by SaveDownloader, if any.
  • Whether the link is from Instagram, Douyin, or TikTok and the public URL format you used.
  • Your browser and device type.
  • Whether the same public post still opens normally on the source platform.

Privacy, cookies, and data questions

For information about analytics, technical logs, cookies, consent choices, and third-party services, read the Privacy Policy and Cookies & Consent page. You can email privacy questions to the same contact address.

Safety reminder: never email a platform password, login cookie, private post URL, payment information, authentication token, or other confidential credential. SaveDownloader is designed for public links only.
