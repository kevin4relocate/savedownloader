Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026.

What SaveDownloader processes

When you submit a public media link, SaveDownloader processes that URL so it can locate media information exposed by the public share page. The service is designed not to require platform usernames or passwords.

Server logs

Hosting and security providers may process ordinary technical information such as IP address, request time, browser information, requested path, and security signals for delivery, abuse prevention, diagnostics, and reliability.

Downloaded media

SaveDownloader is designed as a resolver and does not intentionally create a permanent library of the media you resolve. Third-party media hosts may apply their own logging and privacy practices when your browser accesses a media URL.

Cookies and advertising

The site may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, analytics, consent management, fraud prevention, and advertising after those services are enabled. Where required, visitors will be given appropriate consent choices before non-essential technologies are used.

Third-party services

SaveDownloader may rely on infrastructure, analytics, advertising, or security providers. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies and applicable law.

Your choices

You can use the site without creating an account. Do not submit private, confidential, authenticated, or access-controlled URLs.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The date at the top will reflect the latest revision.

Contact

For project questions or privacy feedback, use the public project contact link in the footer.
