Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026.

This policy explains the information that may be processed when you use SaveDownloader and how the service is designed to limit unnecessary data collection.

Information you submit

When you paste a public Douyin link, SaveDownloader processes that URL so the resolver can identify the public post and locate media data made available through the public web. Do not submit private, confidential, authenticated, or access-controlled URLs.

Account information

SaveDownloader does not require you to create a site account and does not ask for your Douyin username or password.

Technical and security logs

Our hosting, delivery, and security infrastructure may process ordinary technical information such as IP address, request time, requested path, user-agent or browser information, network information, and security signals. This information may be used for service delivery, abuse prevention, diagnostics, rate limiting, and reliability.

Downloaded media

SaveDownloader is designed as a media resolver rather than a permanent video library. When a public video is resolved, your browser may download the media directly from a third-party media host. Those third-party hosts may process requests according to their own privacy practices.

Cookies and local storage

The core downloader does not require a SaveDownloader user account. Cookies or similar technologies may be introduced for essential functionality, security, analytics, consent management, or advertising. Where applicable, consent choices will be provided before non-essential technologies are used.

Analytics and advertising

SaveDownloader may use analytics and advertising services in the future. If those services are enabled, this policy will be updated as needed and applicable consent mechanisms will be used where required. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies subject to their own policies and applicable law.

Third-party services

The website relies on third-party infrastructure and public media services to operate. Those providers may independently process technical request information. SaveDownloader does not control third-party websites or media delivery networks.

Data retention

SaveDownloader does not intentionally create a permanent server-side archive of the videos users resolve. Technical logs may be retained by infrastructure providers for operational, security, or legal purposes according to their settings and policies.

Children and sensitive information

Do not submit personal, confidential, or sensitive information through the downloader. The service is intended to process public media URLs, not private personal data.

Your choices

You can use the core downloader without creating an account. You may also choose not to submit a URL. Browser and device settings may provide additional controls for cookies, downloads, and site data.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change as SaveDownloader adds or changes features, analytics, advertising, or infrastructure. The revision date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or feedback, use the Contact page.
