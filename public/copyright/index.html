Copyright and rights requests

Last updated: August 28, 2026.

SaveDownloader does not claim ownership of third-party media. Copyright and other rights remain with the applicable creators and rights holders.

What SaveDownloader provides

SaveDownloader is designed to resolve supported public Instagram, Douyin, and TikTok media information in response to a URL submitted by a visitor. It is not intended to operate as a permanent server-side archive of downloaded media.

Responsible use by visitors

Visitors are instructed to use the service only for public media they own, created, have permission to save, or otherwise have a lawful basis to download. Downloading a technically accessible file does not by itself grant permission to republish, distribute, monetize, or otherwise reuse it.

Submitting a rights-related report

If you believe the SaveDownloader website itself infringes your rights or facilitates access to material that should not be available through the service, email contact@savedownloader.com.

To help evaluate a report, include:

  • Your full name and a reliable way to contact you.
  • A clear description of the copyrighted work or other right you believe is affected.
  • The exact SaveDownloader page and, when relevant, the public source URL involved.
  • A description of your relationship to the rights at issue or your authority to act for the rights holder.
  • A clear description of the action you are requesting.
  • Any additional information reasonably needed to understand and evaluate the report.

Third-party source content

If your concern is that content remains publicly available on Instagram, TikTok, or Douyin itself, you may also need to contact the source platform or original uploader. SaveDownloader does not control source-platform posts or their content-removal decisions.

Repeat or abusive reports

SaveDownloader may ask for clarification when a report is incomplete and may disregard notices that are clearly fraudulent, abusive, unrelated to the service, or do not provide enough information to identify the issue.

Privacy of reports

Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, login cookies, payment details, or unrelated sensitive information. Information submitted by email may be retained as reasonably necessary to review, respond to, document, or defend a rights-related request.

No legal advice

This page describes SaveDownloader's contact process and is not legal advice. Rights, notice, and takedown requirements vary by jurisdiction.
