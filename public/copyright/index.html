Copyright and rights requests

Last updated: August 28, 2026.

SaveDownloader does not claim ownership of third-party media. Copyright and other rights remain with the applicable creators and rights holders.

What SaveDownloader provides

SaveDownloader is designed to resolve public Douyin media information in response to a URL submitted by a user. It is not intended to operate as a permanent server-side archive of downloaded videos.

Responsible use by visitors

Visitors are instructed to use the service only for public media they own, created, have permission to save, or otherwise have a lawful basis to download. Downloading a technically accessible file does not by itself grant permission to republish, distribute, monetize, or otherwise reuse it.

Submitting a rights-related report

If you believe the SaveDownloader website itself infringes your rights or facilitates access to material that should not be available through the service, you can submit a report through the Contact page.

To help evaluate a report, include:

  • A clear description of the work or right you believe is affected.
  • The exact SaveDownloader page or relevant public URL involved.
  • A description of your relationship to the rights at issue.
  • Enough information to understand the requested action.
  • A way to follow up through the public contact channel when appropriate.

Third-party source content

If your concern is that content remains publicly available on Douyin itself, you may also need to contact the platform or the original uploader. SaveDownloader does not control Douyin posts or the source platform's content-removal decisions.

No legal advice

This page describes SaveDownloader's project contact process and is not legal advice. Rights and notice requirements vary by jurisdiction.
