What these guides cover

SaveDownloader works with supported public post URLs. The guides focus on practical browser behavior: copying the original post link, resolving the post, choosing the available download action, locating the saved file, and resolving the original post again if a temporary media URL expires.

The service does not ask for platform passwords or private account cookies and does not support private, login-only, paywalled, DRM-protected, or otherwise access-controlled media.

Why keep the original post URL?

Video platforms often deliver media through temporary CDN URLs. Those URLs can expire or change even while the original public post remains available. Keeping the original Douyin or TikTok post URL lets SaveDownloader request fresh public media information later.
