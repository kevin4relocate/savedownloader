Cookies & Consent

Last updated: August 28, 2026.

SaveDownloader separates essential browser storage from optional analytics so visitors can make a clear choice.

Essential storage

SaveDownloader may store a small consent preference in your browser so the site can remember whether you accepted or rejected optional analytics. This preference is used only to remember your choice and does not require a SaveDownloader account.

Optional analytics

If you accept analytics, SaveDownloader may load Google Analytics 4 using measurement ID G-07B50P20QM. Analytics helps us understand page visits and product interactions such as successful link resolutions and download actions. If you reject analytics, SaveDownloader does not intentionally enable the optional Google Analytics measurement script through its own consent control.

Advertising

Google AdSense is not required to use the downloader. If advertising is enabled later, advertising technologies may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and—where permitted—personalization. Regions that require an applicable certified consent management platform will use a separate advertising-consent solution rather than relying only on this basic analytics banner.

Change your choice

You can reopen the SaveDownloader analytics consent banner at any time.

You can also remove SaveDownloader site data through your browser settings. Browser controls may affect other storage used by infrastructure or third-party services.

More information

For broader information about URLs, technical logs, analytics, third-party services, retention, and future advertising, see the Privacy Policy. Questions can be sent to contact@savedownloader.com.
