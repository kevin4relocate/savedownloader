Troubleshooting guide

Why a video download fails and what to check

A public link can fail at different stages: the original post cannot be resolved, the media host refuses the download, or the browser saves the file somewhere unexpected. Identify which stage failed before trying again.

Published September 27, 2026.

Step 1: Check the original post, not a temporary media URL

Open the original post directly on its platform without logging in. Confirm that you can view it and that you have permission to save the content. A post that was deleted, changed to private, made login-only, or restricted in your region may not resolve here. SaveDownloader does not bypass those access restrictions.

Step 2: Use the matching platform page

Instagram Reels, photos, and supported carousels belong in the Instagram Downloader. Use the Douyin Downloader for supported public Douyin links, including compatible short shares, and the TikTok Downloader for supported public TikTok posts. Copy the original share link again if text or punctuation was accidentally included.

Step 3: Identify the error stage

The link is rejected immediately

Check that the link begins with HTTPS and points to the supported platform. Profile pages, Instagram Stories, private pages, and URLs from unrelated hosts are not supported. A copied short link must redirect to a supported public post.

The post opens, but SaveDownloader cannot resolve it

The source platform may have changed its public page format, limited access from the service, or stopped exposing usable media data. Try the original public link again later; repeated submissions of the same inaccessible post will not make private content available. If the issue persists, use the contact page and report the platform, approximate time, and visible error message. Do not send private URLs or credentials.

The result appears, but the download does not start

Some media hosts issue short-lived URLs or reject requests from particular networks. Resolve the original post again to request fresh media information. The downloader may attempt a supported server-side delivery route, but success depends on the source host. Do not interpret a displayed preview as proof that a full file can be delivered.

The download starts, but I cannot find the file

On iPhone or iPad, check Safari's Downloads view and the Files app. On Android, check your browser's download manager and the Downloads folder. On Windows or Mac, check your browser's downloads history, configured download folder, and any permission prompts. Browser behavior varies by version and device.

An Instagram carousel ZIP fails

ZIP creation requires the browser to retrieve each supported item and assemble it in memory. A temporarily unavailable item or an unusually large carousel can interrupt the ZIP. Download available items individually instead, or try the original public post again if its media URLs expired. For mobile-specific steps and practical file-size limits, read our Instagram carousel ZIP guide.

Platform-specific checks

Douyin: temporary share links can redirect to a public post. If an older link has expired, copy a fresh share link from the original post. See the Douyin short-link guide.

TikTok: availability can depend on the public post and regional media delivery. Keep the original post URL and check the TikTok guide for device-specific steps.

Instagram: public photos and Reels may behave differently from multi-item carousels. See the Instagram guide before retrying a ZIP.

When to contact support

Provide the downloader you used, your device/browser, the time of the attempt, and the exact error shown. Never send passwords, login cookies, or private account details. You can contact SaveDownloader with diagnostic details.

Responsible use

Only save media that you own or are authorized to download, and respect the source platform's rules. Public availability alone does not establish permission to download or republish content. See our copyright policy and terms of use.
