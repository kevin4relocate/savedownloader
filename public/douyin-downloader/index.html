How the Douyin downloader works

SaveDownloader follows the public Douyin share link, identifies the post ID, and reads the structured media information exposed by the public mobile share page. It does not require your Douyin username or password and it does not attempt to access private posts.

Copy the share URL. Use a public Douyin video, note, or short share link.
Paste it above. The resolver validates that the URL belongs to Douyin before making any request.
Open the returned media. Use the result while the temporary media URL remains active.

Supported Douyin link types

The resolver recognizes common public formats including short v.douyin.com links, /video/ links, /note/ links, public share links, and URLs containing a public post ID.

Douyin downloader FAQ

Does this require a browser extension?

No. The tool runs as a normal website.

Do you store downloaded videos?

The service is designed as a resolver rather than a permanent video storage service.

Can it resolve image posts too?

The resolver can return public image URLs when Douyin exposes them in the public share data.

Can I use it for private content?

No. Private, login-only, paywalled, DRM-protected, and access-controlled content is outside the scope of this service.
