About SaveDownloader

SaveDownloader is a lightweight web utility for resolving public Douyin media and letting your browser save the available file to your device.

What SaveDownloader does

The current public release supports Douyin only. You paste a public Douyin URL, the resolver identifies the public post and available media data, and the website returns a download action for the resolved video.

The service is intentionally browser-based. It does not require a desktop program, browser extension, or Douyin account credentials.

Why the site focuses on one platform

Public media interfaces can change frequently. Supporting one platform at a time lets us test the real download flow, error handling, privacy boundaries, and browser behavior before claiming that a provider is supported.

Privacy-minded architecture

SaveDownloader is designed as a resolver rather than a permanent media library. The site processes the public URL you submit to locate the media. The final media download is initiated by your browser, and the service is not intended to maintain a permanent collection of downloaded videos.

Responsible use

Use SaveDownloader only for public media that you own, created, or have permission or another lawful basis to save. Private, login-only, paywalled, DRM-protected, or otherwise access-controlled content is outside the scope of the service.
