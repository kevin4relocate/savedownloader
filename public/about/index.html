About SaveDownloader

SaveDownloader is a lightweight browser-based utility for resolving public media links. It is designed to stay simple: paste a supported public link, view the media information exposed by the platform's public share page, and save media you own or have permission to download.

Why it exists

The goal is to provide a fast web tool that does not require an app install, browser extension, platform password, or permanent server-side video library.

Current support

Douyin is the first supported provider. Additional public-media providers may be added separately after they are tested for reliability and appropriate access boundaries.
