About SaveDownloader

SaveDownloader is a lightweight web utility for resolving public Douyin and TikTok media and letting your browser save available files to your device.

What SaveDownloader does

The current public release supports Douyin and TikTok. You paste a supported public post URL, the resolver identifies public media data exposed by the source platform, and the website returns an available download action.

The service is browser-based and does not require a SaveDownloader account, desktop program, browser extension, or source-platform account credentials.

Why providers are added carefully

Public media interfaces can change frequently. Each provider is tested independently before it is advertised so link validation, error handling, privacy boundaries, and browser download behavior can be checked first.

Privacy-minded architecture

SaveDownloader is designed as a resolver rather than a permanent media library. The site processes the public URL you submit to locate available media. The final media download is initiated by your browser, and the service is not intended to maintain a permanent collection of downloaded files.

Responsible use

Use SaveDownloader only for public media that you own, created, have permission to save, or otherwise have a lawful basis to download. Private, login-only, paywalled, DRM-protected, or otherwise access-controlled content is outside the scope of the service.
