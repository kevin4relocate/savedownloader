Why use an online Douyin downloader?

An online downloader is useful when you want a straightforward way to save a public Douyin video without installing another application. SaveDownloader runs in the browser and only asks for the public post URL needed to resolve the media.

After a link is resolved, the download is initiated in your browser. SaveDownloader is designed as a resolver rather than a permanent video-hosting library, which keeps the service lightweight and helps avoid unnecessary server-side copies of media files.

Frequently asked questions

Is SaveDownloader free?

Yes. The current Douyin downloader can be used without creating an account.

Do I need to log in to Douyin?

No. SaveDownloader is designed for public Douyin links and does not ask for your Douyin password.

Where is the downloaded video saved?

Your browser controls the download location. Depending on your device and browser, the file may be saved automatically to Downloads or you may be asked where to save it.

Why can a resolved link expire?

Douyin and its media delivery network can issue temporary media URLs. Submit the original Douyin post again to resolve a fresh URL.

Can SaveDownloader download private videos?

No. Private, login-only, access-controlled, paywalled, or DRM-protected content is not supported.
