Why use SaveDownloader?

SaveDownloader is designed as a lightweight media resolver rather than a permanent video-hosting library. You provide the original public post URL, the service locates available public media information, and your browser initiates the final download.

Public platform interfaces can change without notice, so keep the original post URL. If a temporary media URL expires, resolve the original public link again.

Supported now: public Douyin and TikTok media. Other platforms are not advertised until their download flow has been tested.

Frequently asked questions

Is SaveDownloader free?

Yes. The current public Douyin and TikTok downloaders can be used without creating a SaveDownloader account.

Do I need to log in to Douyin or TikTok?

No. SaveDownloader is designed for public links and does not ask for platform passwords or private account cookies.

Where is the downloaded video saved?

Your browser controls the download location. Depending on your device and browser, the file may be saved automatically to Downloads or you may be asked where to save it.

Why can a resolved link expire?

Media delivery networks can issue temporary or signed URLs. Submit the original public post again to resolve a fresh media URL.

Can SaveDownloader download private videos?

No. Private, login-only, paywalled, DRM-protected, and other access-controlled content is outside the scope of the service.
