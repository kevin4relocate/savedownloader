How to use SaveDownloader

Copy a public share link. Open the public Douyin post and copy its share URL.
Paste the link above. SaveDownloader checks the public share data and identifies the available media.
Open or save the media. Use the result link while it is still active; CDN links may be temporary.

Built for speed and privacy

SaveDownloader does not require a Douyin login, password, browser extension, or app install. The website sends the public share link to its resolver only when you ask it to process a link. The site does not intentionally store downloaded video files.

The downloader is intended for public media that you created, own, or are authorized to save. It does not support private accounts, login-only posts, DRM bypass, or access-control circumvention.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. SaveDownloader runs in a normal web browser.

Does SaveDownloader need my Douyin account?

No. The resolver is designed for public share links and does not ask for your username or password.

Why does a media link sometimes stop working?

Douyin and its media delivery network may issue temporary URLs. Resolve the share link again if an old media URL has expired.

Can I download private videos?

No. Private, authenticated, or otherwise access-controlled content is outside the scope of this service.

Will TikTok and Instagram be supported?

The architecture is ready for additional providers, but each platform will be added only after its public-media flow is separately tested.
